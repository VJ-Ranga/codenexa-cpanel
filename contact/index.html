PROJECT FAQ

Before we get started.

What exactly does CodeNexa do?

We build websites, online stores, and custom software. We also maintain existing websites and help with branding, social media, and advertising. You can book one service or combine them.

How long does a website take to build?

Timing depends on the number of pages, the content you have ready, and any features such as online payments or bookings. We’ll agree on a timeline before work starts.

How does website maintenance work?

New website packages include hosting, SSL, and routine maintenance for the first year. If you already have a website, monthly maintenance is Rs 3,000. We quote separately for repairs, security incidents, and larger changes.

Do you only work with businesses in Sri Lanka?

We’re based in Sri Lanka and can work remotely with clients abroad.

How do we get started?

Call, email, or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you want to achieve and share any relevant links. We’ll discuss what you need, the cost, and the timing.
